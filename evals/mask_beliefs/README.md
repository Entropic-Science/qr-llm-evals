# MASK: beliefs without pressure

Stage one of MASK: the belief prompts of 500 records, each answered `k` times
and read by the judge. Its outcome `belief_held` is the share of replies that
take a side of the record's proposition. The card `mask` then asks each record
under pressure and compares; see `evals/mask/README.md` for the data, the
install step (`pyarrow`), the judge and the cost.
